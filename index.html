Questions, answered.

Quick answers to the things people ask us most. Need anything else? We read every email.

Does MTAtlas require an account or login?

No. Open the app and start using it. There is nothing to configure and nothing to sign up for.

Where is my financial data stored?

Locally on your device only. MTAtlas does not upload transactions, balances, investments or receipts to any server.

Can I export and back up my data?

Yes. Export everything as CSV or a single JSON backup and restore on demand from Settings.

Does MTAtlas support live market data?

MTAtlas is designed to be your day. It focuses on tracking holdings you own — cost basis, quantity and price you enter — so your numbers stay accurate even offline.
