Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026 · Effective immediately

MTAtlas is designed around a simple idea: your money is your business. This policy explains, in plain English, exactly what data the app handles and what it does not.

Short version: MTAtlas does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. No account. No analytics. No tracking. Everything you enter lives only on your iPhone.

1. Who we are

MTAtlas is developed and published as an independent product. For the purpose of this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the developer of MTAtlas. You can reach the developer at edible197@gmail.com.

2. Information you provide

MTAtlas is an on-device finance tracker. When you use it, the only information that exists is what you choose to enter:

  • Account names, types and balances you create.
  • Transaction records (amount, category, date, payee, notes).
  • Investment holdings (symbol, quantity, cost basis, current price).
  • Budget limits per category and period.
  • Receipt photos you attach to transactions.
  • App preferences such as theme and currency.

All of this is stored locally on your device using Apple’s standard file system and database technologies. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, and MTAtlas does not transmit:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, address).
  • Device identifiers used to track you across apps or services.
  • Usage analytics, crash reports tied to you, or advertising IDs.
  • Precise location, contacts, or any background data.
  • Financial account credentials. MTAtlas does not connect to your bank, broker or any financial institution.

4. Receipts and photos

When you attach a receipt photo, the image is saved to the app’s local sandbox on your device. It is not uploaded, shared or processed by any remote service.

5. Backups you create

MTAtlas offers an in-app export feature that produces a CSV or JSON file containing only your data. That file lives wherever you choose to save it (Files, iCloud Drive, email, etc.). We have no access to it. If you delete the app without exporting first, your data will be removed with it.

6. Third-party services

MTAtlas does not embed analytics SDKs, advertising networks, social plugins or third-party trackers. It does not require an internet connection to function. There is no remote backend.

7. Children’s privacy

MTAtlas is intended for adult users managing their own finances. The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 (or under the age required by your country).

8. Your rights and choices

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no profile to access, change or delete on our side. On your side, you can at any time:

  • Edit or delete any record inside the app.
  • Export everything as CSV or JSON.
  • Delete the app to erase all locally stored data.

10. International users

Because no personal data is transmitted off your device, this policy applies equally regardless of where you live. If you are in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, California or any other jurisdiction with specific privacy laws, MTAtlas’s data practices meet the strictest available standards by collecting nothing at all.

11. Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy in a material way, the “Last updated” date above will change and the update will be available on this URL. Because we do not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly; please check back occasionally.

12. Contact

Questions, concerns or requests? Email edible197@gmail.com. We read every message and reply as quickly as we can.
