Common questions

How do I create an account?

You don't need to. MTAtlas has no login. Open the app and start adding accounts, transactions and investments right away.

Where is my data stored?

Everything is stored locally on your iPhone. The app does not sync to a server. If you reinstall or move to a new device, use the in-app JSON export and import to move your data over.

How do I back up my data?
  • Open Settings inside the app.
  • Tap Export.
  • Choose JSON backup for a complete restore-friendly file, or CSV per data type for spreadsheets.
  • Save the file to Files, iCloud Drive, email it to yourself, or AirDrop it to another device.
How do I move my data to a new iPhone?
  • Export a JSON backup from your current device.
  • Install MTAtlas on the new iPhone.
  • Open Settings → Import and select the JSON file.
How do I attach a receipt to a transaction?
  • Open the transaction, then tap Receipt.
  • Choose Take photo to use the camera, or Choose from library to pick an existing image.
  • Tap Save. The receipt stays on your device and travels with the transaction.
Can I track multiple brokerage accounts?

Yes. Add as many accounts as you like (checking, savings, credit card, brokerage, crypto wallet, cash, other). Each investment is assigned to a brokerage account so allocation and P&L are per-portfolio.

How do budgets work?

Set a cap per category for a weekly, monthly or yearly period. As you log transactions, MTAtlas updates the remaining amount in real time and shows a progress bar so you can see how close you are to the limit.

Can I use MTAtlas offline?

Yes. The app is fully usable without an internet connection. There is no remote backend.

How do I change the app theme?

Open Settings → Appearance and choose System, Light or Dark. Dark mode is engineered to feel native on iOS.

Does MTAtlas support my currency?

MTAtlas uses your device locale for currency formatting and supports the major world currencies. Set the default currency under Settings.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email edible197@gmail.com with:

  • A clear description of what happened vs. what you expected.
  • Steps to reproduce (as many as you can).
  • Screenshots or a short screen recording if applicable.
  • Your iOS version and iPhone model.
How do I delete my data?

Inside the app: Settings → Reset → Erase all data. Or simply delete MTAtlas from your iPhone — your on-device data is removed with it.

System requirements

iPhone

iOS 16 or later. Optimized for the latest iPhone hardware and the standard Dynamic Type sizes.

Languages

English. The app respects your device locale for dates, numbers and currency.

Permissions

Camera and Photo library, requested when you attach a receipt. Optional and on-demand only.

Internet

Not required. MTAtlas works fully offline and does not sync to a server.
